Key Features

  • Premium Sound Quality – Crisp audio with deep bass and balanced clarity
  • Noise Reduction – Reduces background noise for better focus and clearer calls
  • Bluetooth Connectivity – Fast, stable, and instant pairing with all devices
  • Touch Controls – Control music, calls, and functions with a simple tap
  • Built-in Microphone – Clear voice quality for calls and meetings
  • Single & Dual Mode – Use one or both earbuds as per your need
  • Ergonomic Fit – Comfortable and secure for long listening sessions

  • Portable Charging Case – Easy charging anytime, anywhere
  • Long Battery Life – Extended usage with charging case backup

  • Compact & lightweight design
  • Auto-connect after first pairing

  • Easy to use interface
