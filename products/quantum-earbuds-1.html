  • The Quantum Earbuds package includes the earbuds themselves, a charging cable, a user manual, and a warranty card for peace of mind.
  • Quantum Earbuds deliver premium sound with rich bass and detailed audio. They feature advanced noise cancellation technologies such as Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) and Quad Mic Environmental Noise Cancellation (ENC) to reduce background noise and ensure clear calls even in busy environments.
