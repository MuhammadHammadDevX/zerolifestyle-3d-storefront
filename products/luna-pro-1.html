Luna Pro Smartwatch

The Luna Pro Smartwatch blends modern design with powerful health and lifestyle features, delivering a premium experience for everyday life. With advanced sensors, fitness tracking, and a customizable interface, Luna Pro keeps you connected, informed, and in control—wherever your day takes you.

Features of the Product

⌚ Premium Build Quality – Zinc alloy middle frame with PC plastic bottom case and anti-static zinc alloy frame.

🖥 Vibrant Display – 1.38-inch TFT round screen with 240×240 resolution and full-screen touch support.

🔋 Reliable Battery Life – 220 mAh polymer battery provides 5–7 days of regular use and 10–15 days standby.

⚡ Fast Charging – Fully charges in 2.5–3 hours via magnetic USB cable.

📱 Seamless Connectivity – Bluetooth 5.3 with call support, notifications, music control, and remote camera function.

❤️ Comprehensive Health Tracking –

  • Heart Rate Monitoring (LC09 Sensor)

  • Blood Oxygen (SpO2)

  • Blood Pressure Tracking

  • Sleep Monitoring (22:00–8:00)

  • Breathing Exercises

🏃 Fitness & Sports Modes – Supports walking, running, cycling, skipping rope, basketball, soccer, badminton, and swimming, with GPS motion tracking via the DaFit app.

🎨 Customizable Watch Faces – 4 pre-installed dials, 1 DIY custom face, and more available in the watch face market.

🧠 Smart Assistant & Tools – AI voice assistant, alarm, calculator, stopwatch, weather updates, sedentary and drink reminders, and more.

💧 Waterproof Design – IP68 water resistance, making it safe for daily use and light water exposure.

Specifications of Product

  • Material: Zinc alloy frame + PC bottom + silicone strap

  • Display: 1.38-inch TFT, 240×240 resolution, full-touch screen

  • Chipset: JIELI 7012F6

  • Memory: RAM 640 KB / ROM 64 MB

  • Sensors: LC09 heart rate sensor, Minghao DA267 G-sensor

  • Battery: 220 mAh polymer battery (5–7 days use, 10–15 days standby)

  • Charging: 2.5–3 hours magnetic charging

  • Bluetooth: 5.3, supports calls, music control, notifications

  • Sports Modes: Walking, running, cycling, skipping rope, basketball, soccer, badminton, swimming

  • App: DaFit (Android 5.0 + / iOS 9.0 +)

  • Water Resistance: IP68

  • Extra Features: AI voice assistant, remote camera, DIY watch face, split‑screen, built‑in game, OTA updates

Why Choose Luna Pro Smartwatch?

The Luna Pro Smartwatch is designed for users who demand performance, style, and versatility. From health monitoring to smartphone connectivity, it combines everyday convenience with fitness intelligence. With customizable watch faces, extended battery life, and IP68 water resistance, Luna Pro is more than a smartwatch—it’s your all‑day companion for work, workouts, and everything in between.
