Jewel Smartwatch

Features of Product

Stylish and Durable Design

Exquisitely engineered with a robust Zinc alloy shell combined with PC and ABS elements. Available in two variants—Jewel (with a lightweight profile of 30g/44g strap options) and Jewel SE (83g with a premium steel band)—this smartwatch is built to complement your style without weighing you down.

Vivid AMOLED Display

Enjoy a 1.1-inch AMOLED screen with a crisp 360×360 resolution that delivers vibrant visuals and excellent clarity for day-to-day interactions.

Reliable Battery Performance

Powered by a polymer battery (230mAh), the Jewel Smartwatch offers 5–7 days of active use alongside up to 20 days on standby. A swift charge in just 2 hours gets you back on track without missing a beat.

Health and Fitness Tracking

  • Heart Rate Monitoring
  • Blood Pressure Monitoring
  • SpO2 Measurement
  • Sleep Tracking
  • Breathe Training
  • Menstrual Cycle Tracking
  • Calories Consumption Tracking

Stay Connected

Effortlessly manage calls, notifications, and alerts directly from your wrist. With seamless contact synchronization, call records, and split-screen functionalities, the Jewel Smartwatch ensures you never miss an important moment.

Everyday Tools

Customize your experience using over 300 cloud-based watch faces—with 5 built-in options and DIY support—and choose from 110+ sports modes (8 built-in). Additional smart tools include voice assistant support through Google Assistant and Siri, camera control for capturing life’s moments, weather updates, alarms, calculator, LCD brightness adjustments, theater/DND mode, motion gesture activation, and essential reminders for sedentary behavior and hydration.

Specifications of Product

  • Display: 1.1-inch AMOLED screen with 360×360 resolution.
  • Battery Life: 5–7 days of active use, 20 days on standby.
  • Charging Time: 2 hours.
  • Water Resistance: IP67 rating for dependable durability.
  • Operating System: Compatible with Android 5.0+ and iOS 10+ (supporting Bluetooth 4.0).
  • Bluetooth Version: BLE 5.0 with a 10-meter transmission range.

Why Choose the Jewel Smartwatch?

The Jewel Smartwatch offers an extraordinary blend of aesthetics, functionality, and smart connectivity. Whether you choose the classic Jewel or the premium Jewel SE, its refined design, comprehensive health tracking, and plethora of everyday tools ensure that you remain connected and in control—no matter if you’re working out, working hard, or winding down at the end of the day.
